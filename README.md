# Optical Lattice

NLS Records design archive for the optical-lattice carrier published at https://iama.cc/optical-lattice/.

This repo holds the design text and the physical constraint. It is not a fabrication file for a few-nanometer machine.

## What the page specifies

A transparent three-dimensional carrier: a projected tesseract with an internal carbon-stone lattice. Data is committed as refractive-index variation and Hellschreiber-style pixel density. Multi-axis coherent beams interrogate the volume; interference reconstructs visual tokens and sound-like payloads.

Form factors on the page:

- Desktop dock: recessed multi-axis laser bay, analog-to-digital pipeline, DSP, Noise authentication.
- Vehicle cockpit retro-fit: centre-console dock, live mobility and momentum vectors, ASR envelopes.
- Phone-back unfolding: carbon-fibre panel opens to a transparent tesseract, compact diode and sensor array.

Control class on the page: laser scan, optical reconstruction, ASR envelope, additive and sub-harmonic synthesis, mobility vector, NLS interface, audio DAC.

Source: Jimmy Howe / NLS Records, https://iama.cc/optical-lattice/

## What it is not

A physics optical lattice traps ultracold atoms in a standing-wave interference pattern spaced by half a wavelength, typically hundreds of nanometers. The iama.cc carrier is a visual-music object at object scale: crystal, carbon stone, laser readout, unfolding mechanism.

A shaped air bearing at 1–10 nm (Knudsen number about 13 at a 5 nm gap, mean free path of air near 65 nm) is the disk-head slider. That part is ion-milled in a cleanroom. This design does not specify rail depth, crown, or a flying height, and the CCTV frames used as visual source resolve the street, not molecules.
