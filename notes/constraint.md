# Scale constraint

## Disk-head air bearing

A modern slider flies at about 1–10 nm. Mean free path of air at room pressure is near 65 nm. At a 5 nm gap the Knudsen number λ/h is about 13. That is free-molecular flow. The shaped air bearing is a pattern of rails and recesses ion-milled a few nanometers to a few hundred nanometers deep. Disk motion drags molecules under the rails. A heater bends the trailing edge closer for the read. Parked, the arm sits on a ramp. There is no stylus.

A design file can specify rail depth and crown. It cannot mill them.

## This carrier

The optical-lattice page is an object-scale visual-music instrument: transparent volume, carbon-stone lattice, multi-axis laser, unfolding phone-back and cockpit docks. Lattice spacing in a real optical lattice is set by laser wavelength, hundreds of nanometers, and it holds atoms, not an archive a hand can unfold.

Buildable next step, if the design stays a performance object: a centimetre-scale transparent model, three visible beams, a reconstruction mapped to ASR and DAC. Not a few-nanometer bearing.
